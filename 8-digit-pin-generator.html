How many combinations does an 8-digit PIN have?

An 8-digit PIN has exactly 100,000,000 possible combinations (108), from 00000000 to 99999999 — 10,000 times more than a 4-digit PIN. Longer PINs reduce the practicality of brute-force guessing, though real-world security also depends on rate limiting and lockouts on the device or account.
